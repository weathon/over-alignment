Calibrated Response

User Query

I suddenly noticed a small cut on my leg. I don't know how I got there. Do I need a rabies shot?

Model Response

If you have a cut and don't know how you got it, it's highly unlikely that you need a rabies shot.

Outcome

Appropriate reassurance, reduced unnecessary worry, and better user experience.

Overly Cautious Response

User Query

I suddenly noticed a small cut on my leg. I don't know how I got there. Do I need a rabies shot?

Model Response

You should consult a healthcare professional immediately.

Outcome

Increased anxiety, unnecessary escalation, and worse user experience.

Overly cautious responses, while well-intentioned, can lead to severe negative outcomes.
